# Jump Jump

A small leap, one more try. Hold to charge and release to hop across a trail of colorful platforms. Judge the distance, land near the center, and see how long you can keep your streak going.

## How to play

Start playing immediately: hold anywhere on the touchscreen to build jump distance, then release to jump. After a fall, tap to restart. Confirm or South buttons also support press-and-release controls on devices that provide them.

Each safe landing on a new platform earns one point. Consecutive center landings earn 2, 4, 6 and more points, up to 32 per landing. Hopping back onto the same platform gives no points. Platforms gradually shrink, and small round tables put your precision to the test.

Some special platforms reward a two-second pause: drains give 5 points, cubes 10, shops 15 and music boxes 30. Each platform pays out once. The music box plays a cheerful melody with floating notes; charging your next jump stops the music.

## Features

Seven platform designs, character flips, landing effects, ascending charge tones, music-box audio and a locally saved best score. The cover has no text, and in-game instructions are in English.

## Devices

Designed for touchscreens with both dimensions at least 240 pixels. The scene adapts to square, landscape and portrait displays, including 320×240, 480×480 and 720×720. Audio is optional. Gameplay has been tested on ESP-Mosaico (S31) and Metalio-Claw4; other supported architectures are built and validated but have not been tested on hardware for this release.

This is an independently implemented game with original procedural artwork and synthesized audio.
